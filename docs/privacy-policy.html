BothNow Camera Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026
Developer: Kekeli Tech Media Agency
Contact: +233 26 478 3970 · info@ktmaonline.com

1. Information and permissions

BothNow Camera requests camera access to take photos and record videos. It requests microphone access when you start recording so the video can include sound. You may deny microphone access and record without audio. The app does not request contacts, location, SMS, or account credentials.

2. On-device processing

Camera and microphone data are used to create the photo or video you request. The app temporarily processes the source recording on your device to create landscape and portrait exports. Photos are saved to Pictures/BothNow; finished videos are saved to Movies/BothNow. The temporary source recording is deleted after successful export.

3. User-selected watermark

If you select a logo from your device, the app uses that image to display a live preview and watermark exported videos. The selected image reference and watermark settings are stored locally on your device until you remove or replace the logo.

4. BothNow Gallery and sharing

The in-app gallery lists media created by BothNow in its Pictures/BothNow and Movies/BothNow folders using Android scoped MediaStore access. You can preview, delete or choose to share a selected file. Sharing is initiated only by you; Android opens the system share interface and the receiving app's own privacy practices then apply. The app is designed not to transmit camera footage, microphone audio, selected logos or personal information to the developer. The source project contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

5. Retention and deletion

Photos and completed videos remain on your device until you delete them. You can remove your selected watermark in the app. Temporary source recordings are deleted after successful export. If an export fails, a temporary file may remain in app storage until it is cleared by Android or the app is uninstalled.

6. Security and children's privacy

The app does not provide user accounts. It is a general-purpose camera utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep Android up to date.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact info@ktmaonline.com or +233 26 478 3970.
